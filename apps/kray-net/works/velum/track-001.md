# Dry Margin

VELUM — TRACK #001
spoken word × chamber folk · 78 BPM · D minor

## Description

[Intro]

[nylon ostinato, room tone, no riser]
(spoken, close to mic)
The well is empty.
I write on what is left of the wet.

[Verse 1]

[dry alto, chest voice, cello holds D]
I opened the folio to the side that never took a line
a pale field, a hairline split, the binding still warm
you asked for a letter I had already started
I had already stopped

[Pre-Chorus]

(in Spanish)
el margen no pide tinta
pide que yo me calle a tiempo

[Chorus]

[speech on the downbeat, cello answers one note late]
Dry margin —
I leave you the chair
and the sentence that sat in it
Dry margin —
I do not fill what the page refused

[Verse 2]

[brushes on a closed snare]
An uncut signature waits in the fold
I will not break it for a prettier ending
the cello holds D like a listener who will not interrupt
I count the room instead of the bar

[Bridge]

(in Latin)
margo sicca —
vox manet, os tacet

[cello, two bars, no vocal]

[Outro]

(spoken)
The quill lifts.
Nothing else.

[hard quiet — last consonant, then room, then cut]
