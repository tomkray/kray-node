# Dust Line

OCHRE — TRACK #001
desert blues × field holler · 84 BPM · C minor

## Description

[Intro]

[one buzzing open string, palm on tin, late heat, no riser]
(spoken, gravel, iron in the mouth)
The shade left the yard.
The clay is still working.

[Verse 1]

[dry gravel baritone, no sweeten]
I took the color off the wall with a nail
the adobe kept the scrape, not the pour
a well that learned to refuse still holds a ring
I do not mix what the heat already spent

[Pre-Chorus]

(in Arabic)
اللون يتذكّر
الماء لا

[Chorus]

[field holler, one throat, room answers or it doesn't]
Dust line —
the pigment stays
I lift the nail
Dust line —
I do not pour
I scrape until the dust hangs

[Verse 2]

[tin holds the flattened second]
The hour the wall goes the color of a throat
I count the refuse, not the wish
a rust shirt keeps the yard in the weave
I will not pretty the major for comfort

[Bridge]

(in Portuguese)
o prego sai
o pó fica

[two bars, string buzz only, no vocal]

[Outro]

(spoken)
One short lift.
Dust hangs.

[hard quiet — last grit, then heat, then cut]
