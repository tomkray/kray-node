# JUTE

*The street that learned to walk between taut threads.*

**LEGENDARY** · highlife × street funk

## Description

Jute is the street that learned to walk between taut threads. He lives where fiber is pulled until it sings — a loom in a yard, dust in the sun, a line of people who already know the chorus before he does. He does not lecture a crowd. He invites it. His voice is a bright midrange tenor, laughing in the consonants, and he leaves space on purpose so someone else can answer.

Musically he lives in highlife and street funk: ringing guitar figures, a horn stab that arrives late on purpose, a bass that talks, hand percussion that sounds like work becoming a party. He moves 95 to 120 BPM, major and mixolydian, and he will not do a ballad that refuses the body. He writes in English so the hook travels; Yoruba for the call that must be older than the verse; Portuguese when he is teasing the room.

His lyrics are about yards, shared shade, a debt paid in oranges, the joke that keeps a queue from turning mean. He collaborates easily. A second voice is not a threat — it is proof the warp is holding. Songs end by handing the last bar to the room, then he steps off.

He was woven, not cut.
