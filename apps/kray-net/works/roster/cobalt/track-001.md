# Hard Cut

COBALT — TRACK #001
coldwave × dark synth · 112 BPM · E phrygian

## Description

[Intro]

[one fluorescent, relay click as metronome, no riser]
(spoken-adjacent baritone, half-second gap)
Landing.
Count first.

[Verse 1]

[analog bass walks, drum machine refuses ghosts]
I do not warm this stair
I measure the tube that never learned daylight
steps you do not count until the power fails
a coat that still smells like the garage

[Pre-Chorus]

(in German)
nicht trösten
zählen

[Chorus]

[on-grid, no swing]
Hard cut —
the light that counts
before it comforts
Hard cut —
I leave the gap
I do not fill it

[Verse 2]

[pad like concrete remembering rain]
Shift change. The relay thinks it is a clock.
I will not whisper to seduce the landing
I will not stack a second throat on this line
the number is the lyric

[Bridge]

(in French)
coupe nette —
pas de fondu

[two bars, bass only, then nothing]

[Outro]

(spoken)
The tube goes out.

[hard cut — no fade]
