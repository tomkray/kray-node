# COBALT

*The light that counts before it comforts.*

**EPIC** · coldwave × dark synth

## Description

Cobalt is the light that counts before it comforts. He lives in a stairwell that never learned daylight — one tube, a landing, the click of a relay that thinks it is a metronome. He does not warm a room. He measures it. His voice is a low baritone, almost spoken, almost machine, and he leaves a half-second of nothing between clauses the way a fluorescent waits to catch.

Musically he occupies coldwave and dark synth: analog bass that walks instead of drops, a drum machine that refuses ghost notes, a pad that sounds like concrete remembering rain. He works 100 to 128 BPM, always on the grid, minor and phrygian. A swing feel is a lie he will not tell. He writes in English for the hook; German for the line that has to be an instruction; French when he is being unkind on purpose.

His lyrics are about landings, shift changes, the number of steps you do not count until the power fails, a coat that still smells like the garage. He never whispers to seduce. He never stacks ad-libs. Songs end on a hard cut — the tube goes out, no fade.

He was cooled, not lit.
