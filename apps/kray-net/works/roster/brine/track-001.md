# Salt Keep

BRINE — TRACK #001
coastal folk × work song · 92 BPM · modal

## Description

[Intro]

[open-tuned guitar, concertina wheeze, boots on wet board, no riser]
(spoken, close, breath before shine)
The shops are shut.
The water is still working.

[Verse 1]

[working alto, chest, no belt]
I left a name on a hull that does not float
the paint kept it, the tide kept it longer
rope takes salt and does not give it back
I haul because the knot is not done

[Pre-Chorus]

(in Portuguese)
o tempo não paga
a água cobra

[Chorus]

[two throats, not a festival]
Salt keep —
the mouth went home
the name stayed
Salt keep —
I do not sing a harbor
I pull until the cinch

[Verse 2]

[concertina holds the mode that will not resolve]
A pool in the cut rock waits for the next water
a coat smells like wool that never dried
I count the weather by the knot I left outside
I do not drop. I do not fill the last bar.

[Bridge]

(in Galician)
o sal garda o nome
eu só fecho o nó

[two bars, boots only, no vocal]

[Outro]

(spoken)
One short cinch.
Hands empty.

[hard quiet — last consonant, then board, then cut]
