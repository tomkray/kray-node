# Low Tide

## Description

A cycle for the hour the shops close and the water is still working. Two songs, one rope. The first haul is wool that never dried. The second is the name that stayed on a hull after the mouth went home. No festival. No harbor speech. The silences between the two are part of the knot.

The album is the folder. The songs are the work. Listen in order, then the hands are empty.
