Why this is the easy door — and why Bitcoin cannot do it

A beat is a powerless, domain-separated signature (kray.beat.submit.v1): it says only "I was here," and the reducer can never read it as a transfer or a spend — so even a hostile page could do nothing but earn ₭ for you. The proof of presence is a real SHA-256 (the Bitcoin family), the identity is a BIP-340 signature (Bitcoin's own), the clock is a Bitcoin block hash (unforgeable), and the payout is linear in proven work (Bitcoin's hashrate-share rule — a thousand fake identities earn exactly what one does). Your key never leaves your wallet. A Bitcoin or Ordinals validator must download the chain and run heavy software; here the heavy consensus lives in the full node, and presence is light by design.
