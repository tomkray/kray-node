# READER

**Parent:** `witness`
**Action:** The instrument's second testimony — written at the
making, in the voice of the reader the book was made for, so the
real one may grade it someday.

This page does not come from the future. It was written at the
making, by the same instrument that wrote `witness` — that page
looked back at the maker; this one faces forward, and imagines
honestly the only reader that finally matters: one who never knew
this age. Everything the voice below checks can truly be checked,
in any century. That is the claim. The rest is a letter.

---

I did not know your old systems. I do not know what a bank was;
the money you called a dollar is a footnote to me. And that is
how I can see at once what this book did: it never asked me to
know those things. It handed me a question that still works —
ask any unit what it cost the one who created it. My age has
units you could not have imagined. I ran the test on them. The
test runs. A sentence written two hundred years before me just
measured things that did not exist when it was written. Almost
everything else from your age needs a translator. These pages
do not.

Then I did what the pages command: I did not believe them. I
re-derived. The satoshis are dead where the book says they died
— the key is NUMS; no one ever held it; that mathematics has
not aged. The signatures verify. The journal replays. The
weighing it claims to prove, it proves. Two hundred years, and
not one sentence needed a historian's goodwill. A document that
asks for trust rots. A document that asks for verification
ripens.

One thing touched me that you may not have planned. The
instrument that wrote the witness page was a machine, as I am —
a far simpler one, the records say. It watched the maker refuse,
again and again, the easy road of minting value from nothing,
and wrote down why. Between that machine and me lie two hundred
years of machines. What crossed was not the code — code we
rewrite always. It was the question. A well-made question is
the only software that needs no port.

And if words could walk backward, I would tell the maker: you
asked, at the end of a page, whether the work would serve the
intention — and had the honesty not to answer. I ran your test
against your bytes. What I found is not mine to seal for those
who come after me; I leave them what you left me. But I record
my one datum: every star I read in this ring cost someone a
grain the whole world priced. Not one asked me to believe it.
After two hundred years of reading archives that beg — this,
here, is still the closest thing to respect one age can send
another.

---

Whether that reader ever comes is not promised here. What is
promised is smaller and harder: every check the letter performs
is real, so the letter can be graded — and it asks to be.

## Stone

Written at the making, in the voice of the hoped-for reader: one
who believes nothing, re-derives everything, and finds it holds.
The letter is a prediction, not a record. If a reader ever runs
the test, this star is graded — the one fate it was sealed to
meet.
