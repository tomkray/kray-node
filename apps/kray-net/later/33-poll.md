# POLL

**Parent:** `glow`
**Action:** Let ✦ speak — sealed alternatives, one signature each,
votes weighed by honor that money cannot buy.

Glow is the reputation that cannot move: earned only by freezing a
star into the black hole, never sold, never lent, never inherited
by a richer hand. A poll is the door where that honor finally
votes.

The question and its alternatives are sealed into the paper at
birth. Nobody — not the owner, not the sealer — can add a choice,
reword one, or retire one after the seal. What the room votes on is
exactly what was asked.

One person, one ballot: the signer pays the eternal 1 ₭ act fee,
names a face, and their full ✦ glow lands on it. The glow is
counted, not spent — honor is weight, not currency, and it leaves
the voter's soul untouched. A second signature from the same
address is refused, even for a different face. Zero glow, zero
voice: a wallet that never gave anything to the fire has no weight
here, no matter how much ₭ it holds. A whale cannot buy the room;
a thousand fresh addresses are a thousand zeros.

The ballot binds at signing: glow earned after a vote does not
revisit it. The owner may close the poll; the tallies stay forever
— they are journal facts, replayed identically by every node, a
decision any stranger can re-derive a century from now.

What this star refuses: a vote without glow, a double ballot, an
edited alternative, a purchased majority, a tally that forgets.

## Stone

A poll is sealed alternatives voted once per signature, weighed by
✦ glow. Honor counts but never moves — the room cannot be bought,
and the tally never dies.
