# BORN STRICT

**Parent:** `donation`
**Action:** Keep the mint's cause inside the journal.

A donate that does not carry its own Bitcoin burial is not a mint.
It is a door talking. The book does not listen to doors.

At genesis of a living universe the reducer refuses a
`donate` / `rune-deposit` / `rune-settle` whose SPV proof is not
in the event. The outpoint of a donate is the once-ever name of
that fire. Present-but-false still HALTs. Absent is not "the
operator said so" — it is refused. A cold reboot re-derives the
peg from the bytes alone, or the ₭ does not exist.

The bench may mint without a burn. A chain anyone follows may not.

The door can fail first, named, so a doomed event is never
journaled. That is courtesy. The law is the reducer.
