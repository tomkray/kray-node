# RELIC

**Parent:** `abundance`
**Action:** A written star is unique in the whole universe — the
first writer wins, and no second claim may ever burn for it again.

The living floor is cheap so anyone may speak. Cheap is not the same
as careless. A name is written once. A body of bytes is written
once. The universe does not hold two of the same relic, and the book
must say so before the fire, not after.

At and after the unique-relic pin the reducer refuses a `name`, an
`inscribe`, or an `origin` whose name or whose exact bytes are
already taken — refused BEFORE any ₭ is burned. The maker keeps their
grain; the door returns a clean 409 and the reducer returns the same
sentence on replay. Enforced twice, or it is not law. A taken relic
is not a star to be bought a second time — it is simply not available,
and availability is a fact a stranger can re-derive.

Below the pin a cursed birth could still burn — the fire happened for
the act, and the registry cursed the duplicate after. That history is
not rewritten: it replays byte-identical, because a scar written at
genesis is written forever (A3). The pin does not reach back; it only
closes the door from its own seat forward. Signet ratified it at seq
80, both books near empty, so the law crossed before it could ever be
tested by a real collision.

What this star refuses: a second name, a re-inscription of the same
content, a second claim dressed as a new star. The first writer is
the writer. Everyone after reads what they made.

## Stone

A name and a body are byte-unique in the universe: the first writer
wins. At/after the relic pin a taken name or taken bytes is refused
before any fire; below it a cursed burn still stands, so history
replays byte-identical. A relic that could be claimed twice is not a
relic.
