# RUNE

**Parent:** `bitcoin`
**Action:** Keep a Bitcoin asset from stealing the book's unit.

A rune is an L1 asset on Bitcoin. Casey named the protocol. Anyone
can etch a name, mint a unit, send a stone. That is Bitcoin's
mailbox for fungible marks. This book did not invent it. This book
must not fuse with it.

**One rune is not one ₭.** Minting a rune does not destroy a
satoshi at NUMS. It does not write a journal receipt. Postage on
the etch or the mint is Bitcoin dust — a fee to miners, not a
birth of ₭. Do not sell a mint as an origin. Do not publish a
peg that pretends the two units are one.

**Etch is the nameplate, not the father.** An etch may carry a
pretty mark and a metaprotocol tag. Origin proofs on this book
skip any envelope that is not bare: content-type, and a pointer
if the blessing points. Rune tags, metadata bags, and parent
hops that are not that blessing are not the L1 soul of a star.
The twelve were not born from an etch. A later star is not born
from an etch. A bare blessing may father. An etch may sit beside
it as a name the chain already knows. Keep the jobs apart.

**The book's only mint of ₭ is donate.** One satoshi dies at
keyless NUMS. One ₭ is born. That is `donation` and `nums`.
A rune may enter this house only through the **bridge** — pot or
personal vault, signed exit, payout rebuilt from bytes. That is
pipe C. It is not pipe A. It does not unburn a donate.

Do not invent a second ₭ so a rune looks native. Do not ask the
clock to treat a mint stone as a burn. The self is `kray`. The
mark on L1 may rhyme. It is still a rune.

`bitcoin` is the clock. `satoshi` is the grain. `nums` is the
lock. `bridge` is the split. This star is only the refuse: the
L1 asset is not the receipt, and the nameplate is not the father.

## Stone

A rune is not ₭. Etch is not an origin. Postage is not a mint.
The book's only birth of ₭ is a satoshi that died.
