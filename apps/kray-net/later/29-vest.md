# VEST

**Parent:** `paper`
**Action:** Pour a grant through time — the journal is the clock,
the beneficiary is sealed, and nobody can pour faster.

One beneficiary, one start, one duration, one total — all sealed at
birth. From start, the entitlement grows in a straight line until
the duration ends: vested = total × elapsed ÷ duration, in whole ₭,
never more than the schedule, never less than the mathematics.

The clock is journal height, not a wall clock. A wall clock can be
lied about; the height of the book cannot. Every node reads the
same page number and computes the same due amount, forever.

One door, `release`, and anyone may knock — the beneficiary, a
friend, a stranger, a robot. It does not matter whose hand pulls:
the paper pays only the sealed beneficiary, only what has vested,
only what is still unpaid. `released` remembers every ₭ that left,
so calling twice pays nothing twice.

If the pot is short, the paper pays what it holds and keeps the
debt on the schedule — an underfunded pot delays the grant, it
never burns it. Refill the pot and the next release resumes where
the mathematics left off.

What this star refuses: a cliff hidden after sealing, an owner
clawback, an early pour, a beneficiary swap. The deal readable on
the star at birth is the deal executed on the last day.

## Stone

A vest pours a sealed total to a sealed beneficiary in a straight
line over journal height. Anyone may knock; only the schedule
answers.
