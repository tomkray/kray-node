# ABUNDANCE

**Parent:** `satoshi`
**Action:** Name the two floors — the stone and the living — and why
this book prices a byte in given energy, not in world block space.

Bitcoin already has a data layer: Ordinals. Bytes carved into the
witness of the chain itself, carried by every archival node, priced
by the scarcest real estate on Earth — global block space. That is
the stone floor. It is perfect at what it is, and this book does not
compete for it. It kneels to it.

The book binds to the stone in every blood it has: an L1 ordinal can
father a star (origin — proven from raw transactions, hop by hop); a
rune's whole life on the L2 terminates provably at its own etch on
Bitcoin; a star's crown can be carved back into the stone (the
eternal door), byte for byte. Down, the roots. Up, the life.

The living floor prices a byte differently, and the difference is
the door. Here a byte costs given energy — ₭, born only when a
satoshi dies as a gift — at a rate set so that speech is abundant:
thousands of bytes for the energy of one grain. A whole work of ten
million bytes burns about a thousand grains — the dust in a pocket —
where the stone floor could not seat it at any price, because a
block simply is not that large.

The price of a byte decides who may speak. On the stone, a byte is
priced for the whole world at once, so only the priceless writes.
On the living floor, a byte is priced for one person's gift, so
anyone who truly moves may write — a song, a law, a poll, a game, a
life. Scarcity guards the value below; abundance opens the door
above. Neither floor lies about being the other.

That abundance is the liquidity: when writing is nearly free and
every write is proven, unique, and ownable, markets form on top of
speech itself — stars traded, laws sealed, light portioned, honor
voting. The stone keeps the roots eternal. The living floor is
where the forest grows.

## Stone

Ordinals is Bitcoin's own data layer — the stone floor this book
binds to, never fights. The living floor prices bytes in given
energy so anyone may write; abundance above, eternity below.
The two doors are named beside this star: `origin` (down) and
`eternize` (up).
