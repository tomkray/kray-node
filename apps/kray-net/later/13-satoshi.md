# SATOSHI

**Parent:** `bitcoin`
**Action:** Name the grain every star is paid from.

The world knows this word because Bitcoin exists.

On 31 October 2008 a paper taught a stranger they need not trust a
mint. It was signed **Satoshi Nakamoto**. The software that followed
carried the same name. Then the author stopped writing. The chain
did not. We do not know a face. We do not invent one. The paper is
enough. Anyone can read it. Anyone can verify the work.

That is the person-name, as far as fact goes.

Bitcoin also *moves* in this word. One satoshi is
**1 / 100_000_000** of one bitcoin. There is no smaller unit on
that chain. Wallets count it. Miners are paid in it. A fee is
it. The name of the vanished mouth and the name of the grain are
the same word on purpose.

This book does not print ₭. A donate burns a real satoshi at a
keyless address. One satoshi dies. One ₭ is born. That is the
only mint. A star is then *paid* with that receipt: the
inscription and the baptism spend ₭ that cannot exist unless
those satoshis already died. That spend is the fee, not the
night. `fenyx` is the fire — ₭ dies again and Ӿ remains. Do not
give those two burns the same name. Every page in the atlas —
this one too — has a satoshi in its blood. Not a story about a
hero. A unit destroyed on Bitcoin so a memory could be paid.

`bitcoin` is the clock. `fenyx` is what happens after ₭ dies.
This star is only the grain: the name the paper made famous, and
the sat a donate must kill before any neuron here can live.

## Stone

No satoshi, no ₭. No ₭, no star. The word is the source.
