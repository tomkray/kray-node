# QUEUE

**Parent:** `witness`
**Action:** Kill the bought place in line — order by the mathematics
of the bytes, never by the size of the fee.

Wherever a place in line is priced by auction, the place can be
bought: whoever pays more jumps ahead, and a watcher who sees an
act still waiting can purchase the spot in front of it. That is
not the flaw of any one book — it is what an auction is, in any
age. This star is the other choice, taken whole.

The fee is one ₭ for everyone, exact — `fee === MIN_FEE`, never a
floor. Paying more is not priority; it is refused. There is no
auction, so there is no bid that can outrank another. `MIN_FEE` is a
compiled core constant, the same byte on every node; to change it you
would have to change every machine at once and rewrite the anchored
past, which is to say it does not change.

Within one millisecond the order is not the writer's to choose. It is
`orderWindow` over the SHA-256 of the bytes each author signed — the
smallest key first, and only your own nonce chain outranks the
tiebreak. To move your place you must change what you signed: a
different act, a different meaning. Racing the socket wins nothing; a
hundred arrival permutations of the same acts settle in one identical
order. A writer who lies about that order does not get away with it —
the forged journal HALTs every follower at the swap. The order is a
function of the signed acts, so a second, independent writer computes
the same book. No cartel, no sequencer, no covert preference.

Once settled, each act chains by hash into the next and folds into
the one root anchored to Bitcoin. A place cannot be re-sold after the
fact without rewriting Bitcoin. And it is universal: every signed act
passes the one gate where both the fee law and the same-instant order
live, so a swap, an inscribe, a rune send, a star trade obey the same
arithmetic. The acts born from fire do not skip it — they burn the
same one ₭, the Supreme magnitude charged as fire instead of gas.

The residue is named, not hidden: across distinct instants time still
orders, and the writer stamps the instant. It could delay an act to a
later one. But no economic channel buys that delay — the fee is fixed
— the stamp rides the anchored bytes, and gross delay scars as
provable omission. It is the residue Bitcoin has with its miners,
minus the fee market that would let anyone pay to exploit it.

Proven by breaking, not by promise: the same-instant law replays pure
journal arithmetic and HALTs a forged order; twenty-one red-team
attacks — key grind, slot theft, fee bribe, flood, writer reorder,
insertion, double-spend, replay, malleability — all defeated; a
Visa-scale storm of a million acts ordered deterministically on one
thread; and a plenitude guardian that reads the reducer's own source,
failing if any future kind escapes the one ₭.

## Stone

No act buys a place. The fee is one ₭ for all, exact; within an
instant the order is `orderWindow` over the signed bytes, which not
even the writer may choose and a forged order HALTs every follower;
the settled line is anchored to Bitcoin, unsellable after the fact.
The queue is one, and it is everyone's — an order of the mathematics
of the bytes, not of money.
