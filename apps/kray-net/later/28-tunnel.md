# TUNNEL

**Parent:** `paper`
**Action:** Give a star a keyless pot with one exit — the owner's
hand, or a destination sealed forever.

The plainest treasury a star can wear. Anyone may drop ₭ into the
pot; the pot is keyless, so nothing but the paper can move it. One
working door, `punch`: the living owner names an amount and the
paper pays it out — to the owner, or, if a destination address was
sealed at birth, to that address and no other, forever.

An `open` toggle lets the owner pause the exit without touching the
pot. Closed, the tunnel still receives; it only stops paying. Flip
it back and nothing was lost — the state was flags, the money was
balance, and neither can drift.

Two shapes, one paper:

- **Purse** — no sealed destination. The pot follows the face: sell
  the star and the buyer holds the exit.
- **Funnel** — a sealed destination. Whoever owns the star may pull
  the handle, but the ₭ lands only where the seal says. A donation
  spout that cannot be redirected, even by its own owner.

What this star refuses: a second exit, a stranger's punch, an
amount larger than the pot. A tunnel never owes what it does not
hold.

## Stone

A tunnel is a keyless pot with one exit: the living owner punches,
and if a destination was sealed, the ₭ can land nowhere else — ever.
