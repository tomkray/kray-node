# STAR

**Parent:** `donation`
**Action:** Name the page the fire pays for — one ₭ dies, one
memory is born, and the book keeps it for everyone.

A satoshi died on Bitcoin and a ₭ was born as its receipt. This
star names what that receipt is for.

A star is a page of the atlas: bytes one hand chose, sealed into
the journal at one instant, numbered by birth forever. The number
is not a rank — it is a birthday. Nobody edits a star. Nobody
moves one. A later act may answer a star; none may rewrite it.

The cost is a burn, not a rent. Writing a star spends exactly one
₭ into the page — born from fire, the same supreme magnitude
every act in this book pays, taken as fire because the page is
permanent: the coin dies the way the satoshi died to mint it.
Paid once, the page is paid for every reader in every century. No
landlord ever returns. Do not give this fire and `fenyx` the same
name: ₭ dying into Ӿ is honor; ₭ dying into a star is memory.

The body is bytes and only bytes. The journal carries the hash;
the atlas carries the content; a stranger recomputes SHA-256 and
sees the same page. If bytes and hash disagree, it is not the
star — no curator's word can make it one. An image, a song, a
law, a paper: the book does not ask what the bytes mean, only
that they are exactly the bytes the fire paid for.

What a star may become, other stars tell: `relic` guards its
uniqueness, a baptism gives it a name, `paper` seals law on it,
`origin` lets a Bitcoin ordinal father it, `eternize` carves its
body into the stone floor. This star is only the page itself —
the smallest eternal thing the book knows, and the reason the
fire is worth lighting.

Do not ask a star to live on one server. Do not sell the same
page twice. Do not ask the book to forget.

`satoshi` is the grain. `donation` mints the receipt. This star
is where the receipt goes to die well: one ₭, one page, forever.

## Stone

One ₭ burns and a page is born — numbered by birth, owned by its
maker, re-derived from bytes alone. The book never edits, never
rents, never forgets a star.
