# RAFFLE

**Parent:** `paper`
**Action:** Draw a winner nobody can rig — Bitcoin is the die, the
pot is the prize, the paper is the house that keeps nothing.

Chance without a trusted hand. Seats are sold at a sealed price,
one per address per round. When the round's window closes with two
or more players, the draw happens — and the die is the Bitcoin
beacon already in the journal: a block hash no player, no owner,
and no node operator can choose. Winner = beacon mod seats taken.

The clock is journal intervals, not a wall clock. A round that
comes due with fewer than two players never draws — one player
cannot lose to themselves; the window simply rolls forward and the
seat waits. Two or more, and the field closes: settle only, no
late entries racing the die.

The whole pot pays the winner. The paper is a house with no rake
and no memory of favorites: next round starts with the same price,
the same seats, and a fresh beacon that does not exist yet — so it
cannot be known, bought, or ground.

The owner may toggle new rounds closed; the owner may not touch a
live pot, pick the beacon, or sit in a rigged seat. Their star
hosts the game; the mathematics runs it.

What this star refuses: a house edge, a reroll, a draw from a
beacon anyone chose, a round settled twice.

## Stone

A raffle sells sealed-price seats and pays the whole pot to
beacon-mod-seats. Bitcoin throws the die; nobody — not even the
owner — can load it.
