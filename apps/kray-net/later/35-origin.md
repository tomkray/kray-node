# ORIGIN

**Parent:** `abundance`
**Action:** Let a Bitcoin ordinal father a star — possession
proven from raw transactions, never from an indexer's word.

The stone floor already holds inscriptions. This door does not
copy them. It proves that a living address held a named ordinal's
satoshi, as of a buried block, and lets that proof father a child
on the living floor.

The child is new KRAY content: signed, byte-unique, paid in ₭.
Its body is not the parent's body unless the author carved the
same bytes here. What travels is the bloodline — `l1InscriptionId`
on the star, walkable forever — not the stone's envelope.

Three proofs, or no child:

1. **Existence** — the parent's reveal is a real Bitcoin
   transaction, SPV-buried, envelope parsed from witness bytes.
2. **Consent** — the author signed the origin (or inscribe) message.
   A child nobody signed never enters the book.
3. **Possession** — the sat of that inscription, walked hop by hop
   through ordinary value layout, sits in a UTXO that pays the
   author's address. The walk is raw Bitcoin. No `ord` server is
   law. A satpoint may be a lantern (the wallet that just spent
   the sat already knows it); the node re-walks and refuses a lie.

Replay never re-asks "still unspent" — an old child stays after a
later sale (A3). The live door asks `gettxout` so a pending sale
cannot also bless. Many children may descend from one blessing.
A stolen bag fathers nothing: the holder output must be the
signer's.

What this star refuses: a shallow reveal, a sat that is not the
inscription's sat, a holder that is not the author, a child whose
signature does not bind the parent id.

This door is not eternize. Possession is not availability.
A blessed star may live its whole life with bytes only in the
atlas. The return door is the next star.

## Stone

An L1 ordinal fathers a KRAY star when existence, consent, and
possession are proven from Bitcoin bytes. The child is new here.
The bloodline is the stone's.
