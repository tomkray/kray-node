# BRIDGE

**Parent:** `kray`
**Action:** Keep the bakery from renaming the book.

`kray` already said it: what a stranger runs is donate → ₭ →
stars / Ӿ / Glow. Applications hang on that book. They do not
rename it. This star spends that sentence so a mouth cannot
fuse the doors.

Three pipes. Not one.

**Pipe A — donate.** A satoshi dies at keyless NUMS. One ₭ is
born. That sat does not come home. Donate witnesses the book.
It does not unlock a withdraw.

**Pipe B — the book.** Signature ‖ Merkle proof ‖ Bitcoin
anchor. Bitcoin witnesses the cascade root. It does not create
₭. Honest nodes on the same journal agree. One writer orders
today. That is not a second Bitcoin.

**Pipe C — the bridge.** An L1 rune (or vaulted metal) pays a
pot or a personal vault. The book credits. A signed exit comes
first. A payout is rebuilt from bytes. The user may pay the
miner fee. Paying the fee is not a leaf. On the pot the user
is not a leaf. On a personal vault the product path is still
the book. The CSV after a wait is a named residue, not the
brochure.

Ledger security is not bridge security. A follower holds the
book without pot keys. “Run a node” never means “install the
signer.”

If Bitcoin one day activates a covenant, that rung may lock
where the pot can pay. It decentralizes **this** pipe. It does
not unburn a donate. Do not sell the future opcode as a refund
of pipe A.

The sister thought — lock a sat and mint ₭ that returns — is
another book’s A1. It is not a quieter edit of this one.

`kray` is the self. `nums` is the lock. `donation` is the gift.
This star is only the split: the bakery may go and return.
The book does not.
